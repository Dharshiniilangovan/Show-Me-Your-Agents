# Inventory Decision Agent

## Overview

The Inventory Decision Agent converts forecasted menu-item demand into
ingredient-level inventory decisions.

It uses the demand forecast, recipe/ingredient mapping, current
inventory, safety stock, and supplier lead time to estimate stockout
risk and recommend inventory and order quantities.

## What the Agent Does

1.  **Reads the demand forecast** generated by the Demand Forecasting
    Agent.
2.  **Converts menu-item demand to ingredient demand** using the
    recipe/ingredient mapping.
3.  **Checks current inventory** against the expected ingredient
    requirement.
4.  **Considers safety stock and supplier lead time** before making a
    decision.
5.  **Estimates stockout risk** based on expected demand and available
    stock.
6.  **Calculates recommended inventory levels** needed to safely meet
    demand.
7.  **Calculates recommended order quantity** when current inventory is
    insufficient.
8.  **Supports what-if scenarios**, such as increased demand, supplier
    delays, reduced stock, or increased safety stock.

## Input Files

The agent uses:

-   `demand_forecast.csv` --- forecasted demand from the Demand
    Forecasting Agent.
-   `ingredients.csv` --- mapping between menu items and their required
    ingredients.
-   `inventory_dataset.csv` --- current ingredient-level inventory
    information.

## Output

The agent generates:

`data/outputs/inventory_decisions.csv`

The output contains ingredient-level inventory decisions such as:

-   Forecasted ingredient demand
-   Current stock
-   Safety stock
-   Supplier lead time
-   Stockout risk
-   Recommended inventory level
-   Recommended order quantity
-   Decision/reason

## Running the Agent

From the project root:

``` bash
python3 run_inventory_decision.py
```

The Demand Forecasting Agent must generate
`data/outputs/demand_forecast.csv` before running the Inventory Decision
Agent.

## What-If Scenarios

The decision calculations can also be rerun with changed assumptions,
for example:

-   Demand increases by 20%
-   Current inventory decreases by 20%
-   Supplier lead time increases
-   Safety stock increases

This allows the system to see how inventory requirements change under
different conditions.

## Role in the Multi-Agent System

The Inventory Decision Agent answers:

> **Based on forecasted demand and our current inventory, what inventory
> do we need and how much should we order?**

It recommends inventory actions but does **not** contact suppliers or
place orders. Supplier selection and ordering are handled by the
Procurement/Supplier Agent.
